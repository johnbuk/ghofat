Ullesthorpe 2026

RETURN TO ULLESTHORPE.
 
 
G h o f a t  26  found us all at Ullesthorpe once again, this being our fourth visit to the Leicestershire venue. A smaller Best Western Hotel that, in the past has treated us well with an interesting golf course and a good standard of accommodation. All this and at a competitive cost makes it a "go to" venue in terms of value for money. We had no complaints regarding the service, and the quality of the food at dinner was considered pretty good.
 
Unfortunately the staff in the Pro shop let us down badly, failing to put out our nearest the pin and straightest drive markers on Monday. This did cause a great deal of confusion to us old farts, but we persevered and our excellent Management team handled the situation and order was restored!
 
It was, by any standard, a very small hiccup, but to our elderly members these small issues are increasingly difficult to overcome!!
 
 
Situated in South Leicestershire, Ullesthorpe is convenient to the M1 motorway, and being almost central Midlands is accessible, even though for some it is a fairly long drive. It is impossible to find a location that will give us all a reasonably short journey, if you know of a Hotel and Golf course that will give us all the same drive time, please let me know, it probably isn't on this Planet !!
 
Due to family health problems, Bob Fell was unable to join us for golf on Monday, but, making a sterling effort. he joined us for dinner in the evening. We were all so pleased that he turned up with the intention to play golf the following day, and Bob was able to meet his friends, not only from GHOFAT, but our guests from LGS in Spain and to get up to date on all the latest news from Espanol. Unfortunately, as it turned out, Bob was unable to play golf on Tuesday as he developed what we think is a stomach bug, and he returned home on Tuesday morning.
 
Generally GHOFAT 26 proved to be an enjoyable trip into old farts golf, and it was even more enjoyable as the weather proved to be kind to us. The threatened rain ( that we all know is badly needed) didn't arrive until Wednesday and Thursday, and, as of this five minutes, is absolutely bucketing down here in Newark.
 
We were pleased to welcome five guests this year, Kevin Waterland, recently returned to the UK after 22 years living in Spain, Roger Jennings from LGS in Spain, David Lawson also from LGS, Paul Stanley and John Sail, both from Belton Woods.
 
The very essence of GHOFAT is to keep friendships alive, even though living miles apart, this year Kevin was able to meet up with past LGS friends and enjoy a couple of  days of mildly competitive golf with old friends. Remember that the Golf is only the catalyst to have a reason to meet up, it is why we dreamed up this mad idea 28 years ago, and it seems to be working!

Thank you all for turning up at Ullesthorpe, especially our guests. without golfers, there would be no GHOFAT .
